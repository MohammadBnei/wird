Core sense

To bind fast; to hold a thing to its place. Patience is the rope, not the mood — the self tied to its post while the pull continues.

The same three letters name the bitter aloe (ṣabr), the hard stony ground (ṣubra), and the edge of a vessel (ṣubr). Bitterness, hardness and a held border sit inside the word you recite.

Imperative, addressed to the Prophet. The rope handed over as a command.

Tafsir · 103:3
Al-Ṭabarīd. 310 / 923

Reads the four clauses as one condition in four parts: the exception from loss is not belief alone but belief that acts and then speaks.

Ibn Kathīrd. 774 / 1373

Takes the mutual counsel as the proof of sincerity — belief kept to oneself is untested.

Al-Rāzīd. 606 / 1210

Asks why patience comes last and answers that counselling truth draws resistance — the fourth clause guards the third.
