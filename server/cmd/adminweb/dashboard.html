This page shows totals and what readers chose to send. It cannot show one reader's notes, progress, or when they prayed — not as a setting, but because nothing it can ask the store has a reader in it. That is a claim about this page, and it is the only one made here. The database under it is a different matter: somebody with a SQL prompt can still work out who sent a report, and ADR-0004 says exactly how and why five attempts to close that did not. A report is kept only to the day it was written, because the minute it was written named its author outright.
